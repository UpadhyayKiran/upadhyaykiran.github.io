SSRN PREPRINT · OCTOBER 2026

When Is More
Reasoning Worth It?

Fallback-Aware Reasoning-Strategy Routing Under Hard Token Budgets

I study how to select reasoning strategies when output-token budgets are hard constraints. The study examines frozen routing policies, conservative resource prediction, and what happens when no strategy is predicted feasible.

2,400routed executions
4,000exhaustive executions
4reasoning benchmarks

Across low-to-high budget tiers, macro budget-violation rates fall from 0.258 to 0.033 for Qwen and 0.225 to 0.038 for Phi. These are budget-tier comparisons; conservative routing is not uniformly beneficial. This paper study is separate from the public software release's integration runs.
